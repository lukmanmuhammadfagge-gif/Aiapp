Babi na 1: Menene Kirkirarriyar Basira (AI)?

Danna nan don karantawa...

Kirkirarriyar Basira (Artificial Intelligence - AI) wata fasaha ce da ke koya wa na'urori da kwamfuta yin tunani da aiki kamar ɗan adam. A yau, AI tana taimaka mana wajen fassara harsuna, rubuta saƙonni, gano cututtuka, da sarrafa bayani cikin sauƙi da hanzari.

Babi na 2: AI wajen Koyo da Karatu

Danna nan don karantawa...

AI tana taka muhimmiyar rawa wajen haɓaka harkar ilimi. Dalibai da malamai suna amfani da manhajojin AI wajen neman bincike, tsara darussa, fassara littattafai, da samun amsoshin tambayoyi masu wahala a kowane lokaci.

Babi na 3: AI a Ƙananan Kasuwanci da Kasuwancin Intanet

Danna nan don karantawa...

AI na taimakawa wajen sarrafa abokan kasuwanci (CRM), gudanar da tallan intanet cikin sauri, da rage ɓata lokaci wajen gudanar da ayyukan yau da kullum.

Babi na 4: AI a Ilimin Noma na Zamani

Danna nan don karantawa...

Amfani da AI wajen duba yanayi, kula da ingancin ƙasa, binciken cututtukan amfanin gona da dabbobi, da dabarun rage asara a harkar noma.

Babi na 5: Dabarun Rubuta Umarni (Prompting Skills)

Danna nan don karantawa...

Koyon dabarun yi wa AI magana cikin nasara (Context, Task, Instruction) da kuma kiyaye kure-kuran da ake yi wajen rubuta umarni.

Babi na 6: Ƙirƙirar Abun Ciki (Content Creation)

Danna nan don karantawa...

Dabarun rubuta maƙalu, gyara sauti (audio) da bidiyo, da kuma haɓaka shafukan sada zumunta amfani da fasahar AI.

Babi na 7: Hanyoyin Samun Ƙudi da AI

Danna nan don karantawa...

Yadda za ka yi ayyukan freelancing (rubutu, fassara, zane), gina sana'o'i na kanka, da gudanar da ayyuka da yawa a kan ƙankanin lokaci.

Babi na 8: Tsaro, Dokoki, da Halayen Ƙwarai

Danna nan don karantawa...

Kiyaye sirrin bayanai, fahimtar kuskure ko banbanci na AI (Bias), da kuma dokokin haƙƙin mallaka (Copyright).

Babi na 9: Kalubale da Tsammani nan Gaba

Danna nan don karantawa...

Sauye-sauyen da za su faru a kasuwar aiki da kuma hasashen inda fasahar AI ke dosa a shekaru masu zuwa.

Babi na 10: Tsarin Aiki na Kwana 30 (Action Plan)

Danna nan don karantawa...

Tsarin mako 4 na mataki-daga-mataki domin koyo, gwaji, inganta sana'a, da gina hanyar amfani da AI ta dindindin.
